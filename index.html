Honest limits

Because processing runs locally on your machine's hardware, strict physical limits apply:

  • Optimized for images & standard text files. Excessive files (>80MB) will exhaust browser tab RAM.
  • Proprietary binary Office docs (DOCX, XLSX, PPTX) and heavy video containers are not parsed.
  • PDF is an instant export format (rendering pages locally to PDF), not an interactive PDF editor.
  • WebP & AVIF output compatibility depends on your browser’s canvas engine.
